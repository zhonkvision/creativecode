About This Tool

I discovered Grainrad.com — a really cool free tool that converts images into interactive 3D scenes using WebGPU. I loved it so much that I built this bulk exporter on top of it to process multiple images at once.

This tool is 100% free because the original Grainrad is free, and I believe creative tools should be accessible to everyone. No paywalls, no subscriptions, no BS.
