# Echo of Aria

A Pen created on CodePen.

Original URL: [https://codepen.io/8binami/pen/gbwBxVL](https://codepen.io/8binami/pen/gbwBxVL).

A zen interactive audio experience inspired by C418's Minecraft ambient tracks (Sweden, Aria Math). Move your mouse to play soft piano melodies over A minor pentatonic. Click to trigger resonant arpeggios. Use keyboard pads (A/S/D/F/G/H or Q/S/D/F/G/H on AZERTY) to play deep chords with intelligent stereo panning. Built with ChiptuneSynth 3.1.0 — 4 tracks: piano melody, violin background pad, contrabass drone, and a dedicated loud chord track with per-track compressors to prevent saturation. Global polyphony guard keeps everything clean even under heavy input. Visual: bamboo forest, moon, cherry petals, mist, water reflections.
